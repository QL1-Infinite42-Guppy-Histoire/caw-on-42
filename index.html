CAW on 42

A community discussion about the possibility of deploying a new CAW on 42 Chain — with a community-led CawActions (the contract that records posts and actions) that no single owner controls.

Developed by the community Not an official site Planning / discussion stage
“On QL1, it was BE. On Ethereum, it was Gilgamesh. Both left their communities with a question before walking away. The answer is 42. What do you think?”
— The pinned charter text of the QL1 CAW SocialFi Telegram group

WILL YOU SEE A NIGHTMARE, OR…? IT DEPENDS ON THE COMMUNITY'S ACTIONS…

Why 42

On QL1, the CawActions contract belongs to BE (backend69): its source code is not public, and it sits under his owner authority. The community can use it, but cannot verify it or change it.

The premise of this discussion: BE is not expected to act. Waiting for him is not a path.

BE deliberately left room — an unfinished margin — and a question for the community. That margin, too, is part of his legacy. The answer the community is discussing is 42.

If the action ledger (the record of posts and actions) moves to 42 Chain, CAW would no longer run on BE's closed-source CawActions — a community-led CawActions would take its place.

Ideas under discussion

  • Community-led contracts. future is M🌙NE (@QomLayerOne) announced in the QL1 CAW SocialFi group that her CAW Social contracts are in testing on Infinite Drive (42 Chain), and that she will announce there when the production version is live. The Guppy's Guide frontend has replied that it will adopt her contracts — “please wait for the implementation.”
  • Names — the plan. The plan: holders of a QL1 Name NFT will receive the same name on 42 Chain (free of charge). This is a plan — the distribution has not happened yet, and how it will be carried out is still being worked out. (Free of charge is the public commitment made in the group.)
  • One ledger. Under the proposal, new posts and actions would converge on the new 42-side CawActions as the single canonical ledger. QL1 would not disappear — its names, community and history would stay, and its existing CawActions would remain readable as the historical record.
Honest status note: work on a separate, self-built version on the Guppy's Guide side was started, and is currently on hold.

Token design — under discussion

The token design itself needs rethinking — but no new token will be deployed. This is at the planning stage, in discussion with @QomLayerOne.

The details have not been decided yet. Everything in this section is a direction being considered — there is no concrete specification: no ratios, no rates, no caps, no steps, and no dates.

An idea under consideration (not decided): a merge mechanism for the existing tokens — the Ethereum-edition and QL1-edition CAW. A merge is technically possible; the real design question is the exchange ratio. One possible shape is to build it using the Ethereum-edition CAW deployed on 42 Chain.

  • A token merge mechanism of our own designIn planning
  • A mechanism to mint new CAW by burning eCAW or qCAWIn planning

Both items above are plans only — not decided, and not implemented.

An open problem, stated plainly: BE controls a large part of the qCAW supply. This is unresolved.

Status: planning / discussion stage — nothing has launched

There is no contract to use yet, and no date has been promised — the deployment itself has not been decided. What exists today: the public discussion, the design notes, and her contracts in testing. Next steps depend on the discussion.

なぜ 42 か

QL1 では、CawActions コントラクトは BE（backend69）のものです。ソースコードは公開されておらず、 彼のオーナー権限の下にあります。コミュニティは使うことはできても、検証したり変更したりはできません。

この議論の前提：BE が動くことは期待できません。彼を待ち続けることは、道ではありません。

BE は意図的に余白——未完成の部分——と、コミュニティへの問いを残しました。その余白もまた、 彼の遺産の一部です。コミュニティが議論している答えは 42 です。

アクションの台帳（投稿やアクションの記録）を 42 Chain に移せば、CAW は BE の非公開の CawActions の上では動かなくなります。代わりに、コミュニティ主導の CawActions が置かれる ことになります。

議論されている案

  • コミュニティ主導のコントラクト。future is M🌙NE（@QomLayerOne）は QL1 CAW SocialFi グループで、自身の CAW Social コントラクトが Infinite Drive（42 Chain）で テスト中であること、本番版が公開されたらグループで知らせることを発表しました。Guppy's Guide の フロントエンド側は、そのコントラクトを採用すると返答しています——「実装をお待ちください」。
  • 名前についての計画。計画：QL1 の Name NFT を持っている人には、42 Chain 上で 同じ名前を配布する計画です（無料）。まだ実施されておらず、配布の方法などの詳細はこれから 詰めます。（無料であることは、グループで公約された内容です。）
  • 台帳は一本。案では、新しい投稿とアクションは、42 側の新しい CawActions に 正本として集約されます。QL1 が消えるわけではありません——名前、コミュニティ、履歴は残り、 既存の CawActions は過去の記録として読み続けられます。
正直な現状メモ：Guppy's Guide 側でも独自版の構築を始めましたが、 現在は保留中です。
